# Neurotic Reverb

An additional algorithm for the Neurotic module following the same patterns as the existing algorithms, based on two delay lines.

It will be a two channel reverb.

CV1 and Knob 1 control overall delay time with a range of 0.125:4, log scaled
CV2 and Knob 2 will control feedback amount (percentage)

Menu items will be :

Cross : crossover from the output of delay line 1 into delay line 2 and vice versa
Taps : the number of taps in the delay lines, 1-10, at prime number intervals
Tilt : emphasis scaling between the longer and shorter delays
